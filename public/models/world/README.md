# World 3D Models (`/models/world/`)

This directory houses the 3D GLB models streamed into the Barkshire world by `src/game/worldModels.ts`.

### Assets and Placements:

1. **`super_market_for_free.glb`**
   - Location: Supermarket on the west commercial lane (`x: -64, z: 36`)
   - Role: Large shopping center with solid collision box.

2. **`dae_diorama_-_grandmas_house.glb`**
   - Location: Grandma's House diorama in south meadow (`x: 7, z: 54`)
   - Role: Quaint residential home with porch and garden.

3. **`taiwan_yangmingshan_-_datun_nature_park.glb`**
   - Location: Nature park diorama chunk on the east ridge (`x: 64, z: 52`)
   - Role: Natural terrain showcase with elevation.

4. **`valars_plant_pack_2.glb`**
   - Location: 5 park & town square garden beds (`(22, 36)`, `(44, 34)`, `(-6, 8)`, `(14, 52)`, `(42, 8)`)
   - Role: Dense decorative flower and shrub garden plots.

5. **`game_ready_fruit_asset_pack.glb`**
   - Location: 4 storefronts & picnic spots (`(-11.8, 24.2)`, `(-53.4, 30)`, `(-12.5, -32)`, `(50, -16)`)
   - Role: Fresh fruit produce crates outside grocer, market, and bakery.

6. **`doggy_treats_game_ready_prop.glb`**
   - Location: 5 treat stations (`(-13, -53)`, `(-52.4, 34.5)`, `(-12.5, -18)`, `(26, -4)`, `(9, 50)`)
   - Role: Treat bags and snack piles outside the pet shop, cafe, and park.

7. **`fake_dog_poop_prank.glb`**
   - Location: 5 comedic props along sidewalks and park grass (`(21, 6)`, `(-12.6, 12.5)`, `(-4.5, -7)`, `(40, -26)`, `(-60, 42)`)
   - Role: Novelty props to sniff and inspect.

8. **`oak_trees.glb`**
   - Location: 5 lush deciduous tree groves (`(66, -46)`, `(8, 66)`, `(-38, 62)`, `(32, 58)`, `(-54, -12)`)
   - Role: Wide-canopy shade trees across meadows and park edges.

9. **`realistic_fir_trees_pack_lods_gameready.glb`**
   - Location: 4 evergreen pine groves (`(74, 20)`, `(-26, 72)`, `(52, 64)`, `(-68, 12)`)
   - Role: Tall pine clusters bordering the forest horizon.

10. **`fantasyassets_-_vol3_trees_and_bushes.glb`**
    - Location: 4 tree and shrub arrangements (`(56, 52)`, `(2, -46)`, `(-22, -48)`, `(62, -10)`)
    - Role: Stylized foliage adding biome variety.

11. **`tree_animate.glb`**
    - Location: 5 animated trees (`(6, 18)`, `(46, -8)`, `(60, 38)`, `(28, -32)`, `(32, 22)`)
    - Role: Gently swaying animated trees driven by Three.js AnimationMixer.

12. **`city_street_3.glb`**
    - Location: Northwest backdrop (`x: -102, z: -56`)
    - Role: Distant skyline backdrop beyond the playable world.

13. **`neighbourhood_city_modular_lowpoly.glb`**
    - Location: North horizon (`x: 4, z: -112`)
    - Role: Distant neighborhood backdrop.

14. **`westside_vet_hospital.glb`**
    - Location: West horizon (`x: -94, z: 60`)
    - Role: Distant veterinary campus backdrop.

15. **`old_city.glb`**
    - Location: East horizon (`x: 100, z: 62`)
    - Role: Distant historic city skyline.

16. **`flowersrose_and_sunflower.glb`**
    - Location: 18 procedural plant scatter instances throughout park & meadow turf.

17. **`realistic_hd_yellow_bush_lupine_1525.glb`**
    - Location: 14 procedural lupine clusters scattered across the park.

18. **`grass_claster__downoad__like_please.glb`**
    - Location: 28 procedural grass tuft clusters across park lawns.

*Note: The game includes procedural fallbacks for all structures and flora, ensuring full playability even before GLB files are downloaded.*
